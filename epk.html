ARTIST PROFILE

Presence.
Musicianship.
Connection.

Charmin Greene is a saxophonist and recording artist whose sound moves naturally across jazz, R&B, pop and gospel. Her work is built around expressive phrasing, strong musical instincts and the ability to create memorable moments in both intimate and large-scale settings.

Available for selected featured appearances, concerts, festivals, private engagements, weddings, corporate events and collaborative artist projects, Charmin also brings a musician-first perspective to horn arranging for recordings and live productions.
