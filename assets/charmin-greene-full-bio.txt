Charmin Greene is an American saxophonist and recording artist whose musical voice blends jazz, R&B, pop and gospel with expressive phrasing, refined presentation and a distinctive live presence.

Her career includes concert, festival, private-event and collaborative performance work, with selected shared-stage and event associations including Norman Brown, Richard Elliot, Howard Hewett, LeToya Luckett, Tank, Brandy, Women in Jazz and Urban Music Festival. Her work extends from intimate settings to major live productions, destination engagements and artist collaborations.

As a recording artist, Charmin's catalog includes releases such as Sweet Liberty, Humility, Charmin Greene Christmas and Wide Open. In addition to featured saxophone performance, she provides horn arranging and musical support for recordings and live productions.

Charmin is available for selected featured appearances, concerts, festivals, weddings, corporate events, private and luxury engagements, international opportunities, recording projects and brand/editorial collaborations.
